## Research Interests

**Aqueous Batteries**  
Novel rechargeable battery chemistries, multielectron redox processes, and electrode-electrolyte interactions.

**Redox Reactions**  
Electron-transfer processes, reaction pathways, and reaction reversibility in emerging energy storage systems.

**Electrochemical Analysis**  
Electrochemical mechanisms investigated using in situ spectroscopy, synchrotron-based techniques, Raman spectroscopy, X-ray diffraction, and UV-Vis spectroscopy.

## Research Experience

**In situ characterization and electrochemical methods**  
Development of in situ experimental setups for synchrotron measurements, Raman spectroscopy, UV-Vis spectroscopy, X-ray diffraction, and infrared spectroscopy. Experience with synchrotron X-ray absorption, XRD, and SAXS measurements.

**Materials characterization and data analysis**  
Experience with TOF-SIMS, XPS, SEM, Raman, FTIR, UV-Vis, and NMR measurements and data analysis.
