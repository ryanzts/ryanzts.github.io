# Research Interests

### Aqueous Batteries

Development of advanced aqueous rechargeable battery systems, with a focus on novel battery chemistries, multielectron redox processes, and electrode–electrolyte interactions.

### Redox Reactions

Investigation of fundamental electrochemical redox mechanisms, including electron-transfer processes, reaction pathways, and reaction reversibility in emerging energy storage systems.

### Electrochemical Analysis

Understanding electrochemical reaction mechanisms through advanced analytical and *in situ* characterization techniques, including electrochemical spectroscopy, synchrotron-based X-ray techniques, Raman spectroscopy, X-ray diffraction, and UV–Vis spectroscopy.
